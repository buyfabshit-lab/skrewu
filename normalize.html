Blank Normalizer The Machine

Same frame, every blank.

Drop the shirt photos in. Each one gets trimmed to the garment, scaled so its real width is right, and centred on the same canvas with the same margin. A print placed once then lands correctly on every blank you own — and nothing touches the edge, so no sleeve gets clipped.

1 · What the canvas is

Think of the canvas as a fixed piece of the real world. Every garment is drawn into it at its true size, so the margin comes out on its own and every blank shares one scale.

2 · What this garment is

House rule: women's shown on a medium, men's and unisex on a large.
Sleeve hem to sleeve hem — the widest the garment measures lying flat in the photo, not the spec sheet's chest width, which is taken lower and comes out narrower. This is the one number that makes the scale real: get it right and an 11" print is 11" on every blank.
