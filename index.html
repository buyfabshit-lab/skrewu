Who We Are

Nobody handed us a seat at the table, so we built our own table.

Skrew U started the way every real thing starts — with people who couldn't get what they wanted from anyone else, so they made it themselves. No factory contract. No investor deck. Just a phone, a printer, and the stubbornness to keep going after the first ten people told you it wouldn't work.

We don't manufacture outrage and we don't chase it either. The drama on your feed is somebody else's business model — it's not ours. We're not here to win an argument with strangers online. We're here to make things, sell them ourselves, and keep the lights on doing it our way.

Brotherhood. Loyalty. The freedom to build something nobody gave you permission to build. That's the whole platform. Everything else is noise.

If you make things, you're already one of us.
